# Landing page (apps/web/app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Scope: the public marketing home page only. Header/footer are
part of this surface. Other marketing routes (features, pricing, docs) keep
their current look until they are redesigned in turn.

## Audience and job

Business owners running small teams, often single-shopfront or service
businesses in West Africa, who want an AI agent on their own site. They are
not designers. They arrive asking: what is this, does it know my business,
what does it cost, and how do I get it live? The action is "Start free".

## Proof and content

Real and shipped: knowledge base from uploaded documents, website crawl, and
PostgreSQL / MySQL / MongoDB / Convex data sources; answers from that material;
lead capture; appointment booking; payment links; realtime human handoff;
three widget styles; per-domain scoping; plan limits (free = 1 domain, 100
conversations/day). No testimonials, press, or customer logos exist — do not
invent them. Illustrative conversation must stay labelled as illustrative.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page is a signwriter's board set for one business — a sign is what
a business says about itself in public, and Ziyarn's agent is the sign that
answers back. Refuses the split-hero-plus-feature-card SaaS template and the
black-outline brutalist poster.

OWN-WORLD: cream wall ground, one deep enamel green owning whole boards,
sign-white lettering, one-shot red reserved for live state, a little ochre.
Big Shoulders condensed caps for fascia, a brush script used exactly once,
system grotesk for reading. Painted rules, leader dots, keylined plates; flat
paint — shadow appears only as the physical depth of a hung board.

STORY: one agent, both jobs; it answers from your material, sells and captures
leads, and hands off to you; it reads your docs, site and database; it installs
with one snippet; plans start free. Every path ends at Start free.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a full-bleed enamel-green fascia board inset on the cream wall:
eyebrow and a red OPEN lamp, three-line condensed headline, hairline rule,
subcopy, Start free plate plus a text link; a cream glazed window cut into the
board's right side holds the illustrative conversation, mid-answer.

FORM: signboard sequence, candidate 6 of 7 grounded structures.
