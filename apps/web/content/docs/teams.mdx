# Teams

A team is a shared workspace. Everyone in it can work on the same domains,
agents and knowledge bases, with a role that says how much they can do.

## Creating a Team

1. Go to **Dashboard → Team**
2. Click **Create team** (top right)
3. Name it — you become its owner and first member

A plan allows a number of teams. The button is disabled when you have reached
it.

## Inviting People

1. On **Dashboard → Team**, use **Invite a member**
2. Enter their email address and pick a role
3. We email them a link to join

If the deployment has no mail server configured, the invite link appears on the
page so you can send it yourself. The link is also under **Pending invites**,
with a copy button, for the whole life of the invite.

Invites expire after 7 days, and a pending invite holds its seat until it is
accepted, expires, or is revoked.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **Owner** | Everything, including deleting the team. One per team. |
| **Admin** | Invite people, change roles, edit the team, manage everything in it. |
| **Member** | Manage agents, domains and knowledge bases. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to the team's agents and conversations. |

Admins manage members and viewers. Only the owner manages admins — an admin
cannot promote themselves, demote a peer, or remove one.

## Seats

Seats are what a plan's member limit counts. A seat is held by a member **and**
by a pending invite, so an invite you have not followed up on still uses one.
The header shows how many are used, and the invite form stops offering a seat
your plan will not accept.

| Plan | Seats |
|---|---|
| Free | 1 |
| Plus | 3 |
| Business | 10 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

## Editing and Deleting a Team

Owners and admins can rename a team or delete it from the **team options** menu
next to the Create team button.

Deleting a team removes its members and its pending invites. It does **not**
delete anyone's domains, agents or conversations — those belong to the people
who created them, not to the team. Because it cannot be undone, the dialog
asks you to type the team's name to confirm.

## Working Together

Everyone in a team sees the team's domains in **Dashboard → Domains**, and can
work on them according to their role. A viewer can look but not change
anything.

## Leaving a Team

Ask an owner or admin to remove you, or revoke your invite if you have not
accepted it yet.
