# Billing

Manage your subscription plan, view usage, and handle payments.

## Plans

Every plan includes the embeddable widget, an AI agent that answers and sells,
knowledge base answers and realtime human handoff. The plans differ in limits.

| | Free | Plus | Business | Enterprise | Custom |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Domains | 1 | 3 | 10 | 50 | Unlimited |
| Team workspaces | 1 | 10 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Team members | 1 | 3 | 10 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| AI credits / month | 100 | 200 | 1,000 | 3,000 | Unlimited |
| Widget conversations / day | 100 | 1,000 | 5,000 | 50,000 | Unlimited |
| Marketing emails / month | — | 500 | 2,000 | 10,000 | Unlimited |
| Catalog products per domain | — | 100 | 500 | 3,000 | Unlimited |
| Widget | Classic only | All | All | All | All |

A dash means the plan does not include that limit at all.

**Custom** is negotiated: everything in Enterprise, plus unlimited emails and
dedicated development support. Talk to sales.

## Upgrading

1. Go to **Dashboard → Billing**
2. Pick a plan
3. Pay with card, mobile money or bank transfer (Paystack)
4. The plan is applied to your domains as soon as payment succeeds

## Payment Methods

### Paystack

The checkout on the billing page accepts:
- Mobile money
- Bank transfer
- Card payments

## Usage Tracking

Monitor your usage in **Dashboard → Usage**:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| AI credits | Credits consumed against your monthly allowance |
| Conversations | Widget conversations against your daily allowance |
| Domains | Domains in use against your plan's limit |
| Products | Catalog products against the per-domain limit |

### Usage Limits

Limits are enforced as you use the product, not after the fact:

- A domain beyond your plan's limit is refused at creation
- A team invite beyond your plan's seat limit is refused, and pending invites
  hold their seat until they are accepted, expire or are revoked
- Conversations beyond the daily allowance are refused for that day
- A widget outside your plan's allowance falls back to Classic rather than
  serving something you do not include

## Invoices

View and download invoices:
1. Go to **Dashboard → Billing**
2. Click **Invoices**
3. Download PDF for any invoice

## Cancellation

To cancel your plan:
1. Go to **Dashboard → Billing**
2. Click **Cancel Plan**
3. Confirm cancellation

:::warning
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period. You retain access until then.
:::

## Refunds

Refund policy:
- 30-day money-back guarantee
- Contact support for refund requests
- Refunds processed within 5-10 business days

## Best Practices

1. **Monitor usage** — Avoid unexpected throttling
2. **Choose the right plan** — Match your needs
3. **Keep payment info updated** — Prevent service interruption
4. **Review invoices** — Verify charges
5. **Contact support** — For billing questions
