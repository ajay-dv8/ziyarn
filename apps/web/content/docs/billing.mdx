# Billing

Manage your subscription plan, view usage, and handle payments.

## Plans

### Free Plan

- 1 domain
- 100 conversations/month
- Basic knowledge base
- Email support

### Pro Plan

- Unlimited domains
- Unlimited conversations
- Full knowledge base
- Priority support
- Custom branding
- Advanced analytics

### Enterprise Plan

Everything in Pro plus:
- Custom integrations
- Dedicated support
- SLA guarantees
- White-label option

## Upgrading

1. Go to **Dashboard → Billing**
2. Click **Upgrade Plan**
3. Select a plan
4. Enter payment details
5. Confirm upgrade

## Payment Methods

### Stripe (International)

Accept credit/debit cards:
- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Discover

### Paystack (Africa)

Accept African payment methods:
- Mobile money
- Bank transfer
- Card payments

## Usage Tracking

Monitor your usage in **Dashboard → Usage**:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Conversations | Total conversations this month |
| Domains | Active domains |
| Knowledge entries | Total knowledge base entries |
| Products | Total products synced |

### Usage Limits

Free plan limits:
- 100 conversations/month
- 1 domain
- 50 knowledge entries

Exceeding limits:
- Conversations continue but are throttled
- Upgrade to remove limits
- Usage resets monthly

## Invoices

View and download invoices:
1. Go to **Dashboard → Billing**
2. Click **Invoices**
3. Download PDF for any invoice

## Cancellation

To cancel your plan:
1. Go to **Dashboard → Billing**
2. Click **Cancel Plan**
3. Confirm cancellation

:::warning
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period. You retain access until then.
:::

## Refunds

Refund policy:
- 30-day money-back guarantee
- Contact support for refund requests
- Refunds processed within 5-10 business days

## Best Practices

1. **Monitor usage** — Avoid unexpected throttling
2. **Choose the right plan** — Match your needs
3. **Keep payment info updated** — Prevent service interruption
4. **Review invoices** — Verify charges
5. **Contact support** — For billing questions
